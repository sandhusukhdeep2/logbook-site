Log Mc — Beta Tester Guide

Thanks for helping test Log Mc 🚚

Log Mc is a simple work & rest assistant for Australian heavy-vehicle drivers. In plain words it tells you: when you can start, how long you can drive, what a break buys you, and where you stand for the week and fortnight — measured against the NHVR fatigue rules.

Please read: this is a test version (beta), and Log Mc is an assistant — not a certified Electronic Work Diary (EWD). Keep using your normal logbook. Don't rely on Log Mc for compliance yet — we're testing the numbers and the screens with your help.

1. How to join (about 5 minutes)

  1. On your iPhone, install the free TestFlight app from the App Store.
  2. Tap Join the beta on TestFlight (the green button above) — or open this link on your iPhone: testflight.apple.com/join/AcHAaD7c
  3. Tap Accept, then Install Log Mc.
  4. Open Log Mc.

2. First time you open it

  1. Tap Sign in with Apple (you can choose “Hide My Email” — that's fine).
  2. Pick the fatigue rule set you actually drive under (e.g. Standard Hours · Solo, BFM, WA…).
  3. Confirm your home base state (it may fill in from your location — you can change it).
  4. That's it — you're set up.

3. Using it day to day

  • Before a shift — the “Before you start” card shows when you can start, how long you can work, your next breaks, and work left this week/fortnight.
  • Start work — tap Start Shift when you begin.
  • Taking a break — tap to go on rest; it shows how much drive time 15 / 30 / 60 minutes earns back.
  • End of shift — tap End Shift, then review and tidy the timeline if anything's off.
  • Driving — tap the lock button for a big live timer (drive time left while working, rest progress while stopped).

4. What we'd love your feedback on

Anything that's confusing or wrong — a number that doesn't add up, a screen that's unclear, or a crash. The most useful report is a screenshot + one line of what you were doing.
